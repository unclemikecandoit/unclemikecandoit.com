From Our Table · No. 01 Ranch

Ranch Burgers & Fries

A simple burger-and-fries dinner seasoned with Crooked Gate No. 01 Ranch from the patties to the potatoes.

You'll Need

  • Ground beef
  • Potatoes, cut into fries
  • Crooked Gate No. 01 Ranch
  • Cooking oil
  • Your favorite cheese
  • Burger buns or crisp lettuce
  • Your favorite burger toppings
Make It Yours

Serve it on a toasted bun or wrap it in crisp lettuce. Add avocado, pickles, peppers, onions, or whatever your family likes on a burger.

Make It Like We Do

  1. Prep the fries. Cut the potatoes into fries, toss lightly with oil, and season generously with Crooked Gate No. 01 Ranch.
  2. Cook until crisp. Air fry or bake until the potatoes are browned on the edges and tender inside.
  3. Season the burgers. Form the ground beef into patties and season both sides with Crooked Gate No. 01 Ranch.
  4. Cook the patties. Pan cook or grill to your preferred doneness. Add cheese near the end if you want it.
  5. Build and serve. Add your favorite toppings and serve with the Ranch-seasoned fries.
